Frequently Asked Questions (Sheger Dabo)

Everything you need to know about Sheger bread (የሸገር ዳቦ), retail prices, distribution, and partnerships in Addis Ababa.

Sheger Dabo (የሸገር ዳቦ) is Addis Ababa’s premier affordable staple bread produced by the Sheger Bread & Flour Factory under Horizon Plantations PLC. Baked with fortified wheat flour, it provides essential daily nutrition, soft crumb, and certified food safety to millions of residents.

Sheger Dabo is sold at designated municipal kiosks, local neighborhood shops, consumer associations, schools, and hospitals across all 11 sub-cities of Addis Ababa including Bole, Kirkos, Arada, Yeka, Akaki-Kaliti, Kolfe Keranio, Nifas Silk, Addis Ketema, Gullele, Lideta, and Lemi Kura.

Sheger Dabo was created to stabilize bread prices and combat inflation for Ethiopian families. Through large-scale automated baking producing over 1.5 million loaves per day, efficiency reduces costs, keeping bread accessible at uniform, affordable government-supported prices.

Licensed food retailers and distributors can register through our 4-step partner program. Simply visit our Kaliti facility with your valid trade license to schedule bulk pickup or daily door-to-door distribution routes.
