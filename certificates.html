Certifications & Quality Awards FAQ

Everything you need to know about Sheger Dabo certificates, ISO food safety standards, and quality credentials.

Yes. Sheger Dabo holds official ISO 22000:2018 Food Safety certification. Sheger Bread and Flour Factory was audited and certified under Registration No. ECAE/FS/007 by the Ethiopian Conformity Assessment Enterprise (ECAE) with worldwide recognition under the International Accreditation Forum (IAF MLA). This certifies that our automated mixing, high-temperature tunnel baking, fortified flour milling, and hygienic kiosk distribution eliminate all biological and chemical hazards to ensure 100% safe bread for Ethiopian families every day.

Yes. Sheger Bread and Flour Factory holds official, verified ISO certifications including ISO 22000:2018 for Food Safety Management and ISO 9001:2015 for Quality Management. These certificates are issued by the Ethiopian Conformity Assessment Enterprise (ECAE) and accredited under the International Accreditation Forum (IAF). Every loaf of Sheger Dabo meets strict national and international food standards.

Sheger Bread & Flour Factory holds 4 major ISO certifications:
• ISO 22000:2018 (Food Safety Management System) — Reg. No. ECAE/FS/007
• ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management System) — Reg. No. ECAE/QS/0155
• ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management System) — Reg. No. ECAE/ES/014
• ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety) — Reg. No. ECAE/OHS/014
All certifications cover the complete scope of "Production, Sales and Distribution of Bread and Flour".

The audits are conducted by the Ethiopian Conformity Assessment Enterprise (ECAE) with accreditation from EAS and the International Accreditation Forum (IAF). You can verify the authentic status of our certificates directly on the official ECAE portal at https://ecae.org.et.

These ISO standards guarantee that Sheger bread is baked in temperature-controlled automated ovens with zero manual contamination, made exclusively from fortified wheat flour enriched with Zinc and B vitamins, and distributed across Addis Ababa sub-cities under strict sanitation guidelines.
